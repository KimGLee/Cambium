## Source Maintenance

- Check for dead links periodically.
- Update the body and `last_verified` when versions change.
- After a canonical note is updated, check the Case Studies citing that conclusion, and the expression-layer artifacts registered by `Expression Layer Entry`.
- For a new source, first decide whether to update an existing page, create a Source Note, form a Research Synthesis, or defer.
- Outdated content SHOULD be explicitly marked, not silently kept.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Core / System pages have reliable Sources.
- Time-sensitive content has been verified online or against official materials.
- Formulas and numeric examples have been checked.
- Conclusions correspond directly to sources.
- Empirical, normative, and theoretical definitions are distinguished.
- Terms in the different languages specified by the `Language Contract` have consistent meaning.
- Reported claims, inferences, syntheses, and recommendations are distinguished.
- Metric conclusions can be traced to their evaluation setup, not left as isolated numbers.
- Vendor-specific experience has not been generalized unconditionally.
- New knowledge has passed the canonical promotion gate.
