## Official Company Source Policy

- Official articles from different companies are primary implementation evidence, suitable for analyzing their public systems, experiments, and engineering experience.
- Undisclosed components MUST NOT be filled in as established facts through common sense.
- When multiple companies adopt similar patterns, whether the tasks, models, execution / control setups, and evaluation environments are comparable MUST still be compared.
- Vendor terms are promoted to canonical terminology only when the boundary is stable, reusable, and the term has passed terminology review.

The selected profile's set of named primary sources, applicability, priority triggers, and comparison and missing-record rules are registered by `Source Policy`.

## Cross-source Verification

When synthesizing multiple sources, check at least:

- Whether they are genuinely independent, or reprint the same conclusion from one another.
- Whether they use the same definitions, tasks, and evaluation dimensions.
- Whether agreement is agreement of mechanism or only superficially similar wording.
- Whether conflicts come from differences in evidence, environment, model capability, or goals.
- Whether vendor-specific implementation can be distinguished from generalizable patterns.
- Whether independent reproductions, counterexamples, or production postmortems exist.

The number of sources MUST NOT substitute for evidence quality.
