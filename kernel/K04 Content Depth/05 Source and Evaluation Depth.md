## Source Note And Research Synthesis Depth

The depth of a Source Note comes from faithfulness and evidence boundary; expanding it into a complete textbook is not required. Depth for Source Note and Research Synthesis is not judged by word count, but by whether claim coverage, source boundary, and graph impact are clear. The canonical definition of the structure templates is in [[kernel/K06 Knowledge Intake and Evolution/04 Intake Note Types and Source Roles|K06/04]].

## Evaluation Provenance Depth

When Accuracy, success rate, pass rate, benchmark improvement, or production-effect numbers appear, the body MUST be able to answer the complete measurement path; the canonical definition of the element checklist is in [[kernel/K07 Sources and Accuracy/04 Evaluation and Source Quality|K07/04]].

Explaining only the metric formula without explaining the measurement path MUST NOT be treated as having explained the system's effect clearly.
