## Purpose

由 `Expression Layer Entry` 注册的表达产物独立于 canonical knowledge 保存，并通过可解析的 wiki links 回接其知识 owners。表达次序、练习材料或评估材料不得污染 canonical notes 的定义、机制和事实 owner。

## Core Separation

```text
Canonical Knowledge = understand and own the subject
Expression Role = express the subject
Practice Role = retrieve and rehearse the subject
Evaluation Role = evaluate the expression
Sequence Role = order the work
```

所选 profile 可以把这些角色绑定到一个或多个具体产物，但不能把角色合并解释为 ownership 转移。Canonical note 不保存完整的派生表达、练习集合或评估集合，只保留理解当前知识所需的最小上下文和指向相应表达产物的 wiki link。

Ownership 的基础规则见 [[kernel/03 Note Types and Ownership/02 Ownership and Canonical Notes|Ownership and Canonical Notes]]。

## Physical Structure

逻辑分类不要求提前建立没有内容的物理目录。只有实际产物需要承载位置时才创建目录；任何目录迁移仍须满足 [[kernel/01 Scope and Architecture/04 Folder and Shared Ownership#Physical Folder Policy|Physical Folder Policy]]，并遵守 [[kernel/11 Expression Layer/07 Expression Migration Audit and Acceptance#Migration Policy|Migration Policy]]。
