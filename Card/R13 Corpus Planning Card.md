# R13 Corpus Planning Card

## Purpose

Create or reconcile the Profile-bound corpus-planning artifacts and hand an accepted semantic gap to Coverage without authoring the resulting content.

## Actions

- Confirm that Corpus Planning is applicable and resolve the three distinct bound artifacts, capability scale, and pass authority.
- Reconcile stable identities and explicit relations across the Global Map, Capability Matrix, and Gap Register.
- Invoke `corpus-plan-structure`; obtain `corpus-plan-semantic-acceptance` when semantic capability acceptance is required.
- Hand an accepted gap to its single Coverage object, then select another route for content work or audit.

## Stop or escalate

- Stop when applicability, artifact bindings, cross-references, authority, or the gap-to-Coverage handoff is unresolved.
- Escalate a proposed change to Profile planning policy or corpus architecture.

## Read-back hook

Resolve `R13:planning-relation` for structural, Coverage, migration, architecture, or audit-scope questions.
