## What This Slot Must Answer

Three things: what language the body text is written in, how headings and labels are displayed, and what unit the kernel's length ranges are counted in. The kernel writes its soft length guidance in abstract units because a range that is reasonable in words is not reasonable in characters, and the kernel does not know which one you use.

You may interpret the kernel's soft length ranges in your own units. You may not change the numeric ranges, and you may not turn a soft length reference into a hard gate — length is a signal for review, never a pass/fail condition.

A filled version is in `profiles/examples/eng-handbook/language-contract.md`.
