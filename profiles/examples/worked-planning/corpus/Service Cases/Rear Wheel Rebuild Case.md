# Rear Wheel Rebuild Case

## Service Case Log

A member's rear wheel arrived with a cracked rim, a dragging hub, and no service history. It was rebuilt onto a new rim with the original hub.

## Intake Notes

Cracks at four spoke holes on the drive side, hub with detectable lateral play, three spokes visibly slack. The member asked for a rebuild rather than a replacement wheel.

## Parts Used

New rim of the same effective diameter, new drive-side spokes, original hub after a cup-and-cone overhaul, original nipples discarded.

## Bench Time

Three hours and ten minutes of bench time across two sessions, including the hub overhaul and two stress-relief cycles.

## Measurements Retained

Final mean tension and per-spoke deviation were recorded and are the evidence behind the working range on the spoke tension page. Dish was centred within the stand's indicator width.
