# Wheel Bearing Types

The co-op services three bearing families, and every service decision starts by
naming which one is in the hub.

- **Cup-and-cone.** Loose or caged balls running on a threaded cone. Serviceable
  with hand tools; adjustable preload; tolerates repeated overhaul.
- **Cartridge.** A sealed unit pressed into the shell. Replaced rather than
  adjusted; requires a press and the correct drift.
- **Hybrid.** A cartridge inner race riding on a cone-shaped adapter. Behaves
  like a cartridge for replacement and like cup-and-cone for preload.

## How To Tell Them Apart

Remove the axle end cap. A visible threaded cone with a locknut is
cup-and-cone. A smooth pressed ring with a rubber or steel seal face is a
cartridge. A cone that turns freely inside a pressed ring is hybrid.

## Why The Distinction Is Load Bearing

The bearing family determines the tool list, the shop time, and whether the
service is an adjustment or a part replacement. Every downstream procedure page
assumes this identification has already been made correctly.
