# Spoke Tension Basics

A wheel holds its shape because every spoke pulls the rim toward the opposite
side of the hub. Tension, not rigidity, is what keeps it round.

## The Three Quantities

- **Absolute tension.** How hard each spoke pulls, read with a tension meter and
  converted through the meter's own table for that spoke gauge.
- **Balance.** How far individual spokes deviate from the wheel's mean tension.
- **Dish.** Whether the rim sits centred between the hub locknuts.

A wheel can be perfectly round and still be wrong: even lateral truth with
uneven tension fails within a season.

## Working Range

Rim manufacturers publish a maximum tension for the rim, not for the spoke. The
usable range is the rim maximum minus a safety margin, and the drive-side and
non-drive-side values differ because the hub is asymmetric.

## What Changes This Page

New measurements taken during a rebuild are the evidence that keeps the working
range current; a rim model whose published maximum differs materially becomes a
separate entry rather than a footnote here.
