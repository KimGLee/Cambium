# Manufacturer Service Documents

What the co-op treats as canonical for hub and rim figures, and where each
document lives.

## Held Documents

- Hub service manuals for the three bearing families the co-op stocks, each
  identified by its printed revision.
- Rim technical sheets carrying the published maximum spoke tension.

## Rules Of Use

- A torque, preload, or maximum-tension figure is quoted with the document
  revision it came from.
- When a document is superseded, every page quoting a figure from the old
  revision is re-checked before the new figure is adopted.
- A figure observed on the bench never overrides a published figure; it is
  recorded as a measurement with its own date.

## Effect Of A Revision

A revision that changes a bearing family's published procedure changes the parts
and tooling assumptions of the service cases already recorded, so those cases
are re-read rather than archived silently.
