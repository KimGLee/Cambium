# Wheel Truing Procedure

Bring a built wheel back into lateral truth, radial truth, and dish without leaving it under-tensioned.

## Before Starting

1. Confirm the bearing service state on the hub; a loose hub reads as a lateral error that truing cannot fix.
2. Mount the wheel in the stand and set both indicators to just touch the rim.
3. Record the starting mean tension.

## Sequence

1. Correct dish first, in half-turn steps, alternating sides.
2. Correct lateral error next, working from the largest deviation outward.
3. Correct radial error last, in pairs, so tension balance does not drift.
4. Stress-relieve, then re-read every spoke and repeat until the deviation band is inside the working range recorded on the tension page.

## Finish Condition

The wheel is finished when lateral and radial deviation are inside the stand's indicator width, dish is centred, and no spoke reads outside the working band. A wheel that meets the first two conditions but not the third is not finished.
