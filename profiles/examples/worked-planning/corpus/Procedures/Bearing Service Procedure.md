# Bearing Service Procedure

Restore a hub to its serviceable state, chosen by bearing family.

## Cup-and-cone

1. Remove the axle, keeping left and right cones separate.
2. Clean, inspect the cone and cup surfaces for pitting, and replace pitted
   parts as a set.
3. Repack, reassemble, and set preload so the axle turns freely with no
   detectable lateral play once the quick-release is closed.

## Cartridge

1. Support the shell on the bench block and press the old unit out from the
   opposite side.
2. Press the new unit in square, using the drift that matches its outer
   diameter; never drive on the inner race.
3. Confirm the axle turns without drag before refitting the end caps.

## Torque And Preload

Every torque figure and preload rule used here comes from the manufacturer
service document for that hub, recorded under `corpus/Source Notes`. This page
does not restate a figure without naming the document it came from.

## Bench Setup

The truing stand and bench block set up in this procedure are the same fixtures
the truing procedure assumes are already available.
